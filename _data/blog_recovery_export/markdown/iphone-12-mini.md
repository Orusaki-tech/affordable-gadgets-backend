## Design and Build: The Compact Pioneer

At 5.4 inches, the iPhone 12 mini was the smallest flagship iPhone of the modern era. Weighing just 135g, it's incredibly pocketable and comfortable for one-handed use. The aluminium frame and glass back retain the same premium feel as the larger iPhone 12.

## Key Features

- **Display:** 5.4-inch Super Retina XDR OLED — compact but vibrant.
- **Camera:** 12MP dual cameras with Night Mode.
- **Chip:** A14 Bionic — still capable for everyday tasks.
- **MagSafe:** Full MagSafe compatibility for accessories.

## The Verdict

The iPhone 12 mini is perfect for anyone who values compact size above all else. In 2026, it's an affordable entry into the iPhone ecosystem with a screen that's still great for everyday use.
