## Design and Build: Flagship DNA, Affordable Price

The Galaxy S21 FE (Fan Edition) was designed to deliver the core Galaxy S21 experience at a more accessible price point. The contour-cut camera housing, flat Dynamic AMOLED display, and sleek design made it instantly recognisable as part of the S21 family.

The phone featured a plastic back (Glasstic) like the base S21, keeping the weight at 177g. It was available in a range of vibrant colours that appealed to a younger audience.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy S21 FE was powered by the Snapdragon 888 or Exynos 2100 — the same flagship chip as the S21 series.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Chipset:** Snapdragon 888 / Exynos 2100 — flagship-level performance.
- **Display:** 6.4-inch FHD+ Dynamic AMOLED 2X, 120Hz. The 120Hz display was smooth and vibrant.
- **Camera:** 12MP main + 12MP ultra-wide + 8MP telephoto (3x). The triple-camera setup offered versatility.
- **Battery:** 4,500mAh with 25W charging. Solid battery life.
- **Storage:** 128GB/256GB (no microSD).

## The Verdict

The Galaxy S21 FE was an excellent value phone that offered flagship performance at a mid-range price. In 2026, it's a great option for anyone who wants a smooth Samsung experience on a budget.
