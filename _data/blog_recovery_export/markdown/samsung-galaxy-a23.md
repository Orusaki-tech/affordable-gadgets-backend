## Design and Build: Mature and Refined

The Galaxy A23 adopted a more mature design language compared to its predecessors, with a clean back panel and a rectangular camera housing that housed the four lenses. The flat display with a V-notch cutout and relatively slim bezels gave it a modern silhouette.

The side-mounted fingerprint sensor was fast and convenient, and the phone's 195g weight was well-balanced. Samsung retained the 3.5mm headphone jack and USB-C port.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy A23 introduced a 120Hz display to the budget segment — a significant upgrade that made the phone feel incredibly smooth.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 6.6-inch FHD+ PLS LCD, 120Hz. The 120Hz refresh rate delivers buttery-smooth scrolling that rivals flagship phones.
- **Camera:** 50MP main with OIS + 5MP ultra-wide + 2MP macro + 2MP depth. The 50MP sensor with OIS captures sharp, stable images even in challenging conditions.
- **Battery:** 5,000mAh with 25W charging. Fast charging and massive capacity.
- **Processor:** Snapdragon 680 (4G) or Snapdragon 695 (5G).
- **Storage:** 64GB/128GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy A23 was a game-changer in the budget segment. The 120Hz display made everyday use feel premium, and the 50MP OIS camera delivered reliable results. It's an excellent choice for anyone who values display smoothness on a budget.
