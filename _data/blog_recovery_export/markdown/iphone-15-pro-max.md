## Design and Build: Titanium Flagship

The iPhone 15 Pro Max was Apple's first titanium Max — and the difference was dramatic. At 221g, it was significantly lighter than the 14 Pro Max's 240g stainless steel frame. The curved edges and brushed titanium finish made it feel more refined in the hand.

This was also the first iPhone to feature a periscope-style telephoto lens, enabling 5x optical zoom. The camera bump is larger than the 15 Pro's, accommodating the tetraprism lens assembly.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The A17 Pro chip powers the 15 Pro Max with ease, delivering flagship-level performance for years to come.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Camera:** 48MP main + 12MP ultra-wide + 12MP periscope telephoto with 5x optical zoom. The telephoto lens captures crisp, detailed shots of distant subjects.
- **Display:** 6.7-inch LTPO OLED with 120Hz ProMotion. Stunning for media consumption.
- **Battery:** Up to 29 hours of video playback — still competitive with current flagships.
- **Build:** Titanium frame, IP68, USB-C with USB 3 speeds.

## Who Is the iPhone 15 Pro Max Actually For?

The iPhone 15 Pro Max is for anyone who wants flagship zoom capabilities at a reduced price. The 5x optical zoom is genuinely useful for travel, wildlife, and sports photography.

## The Verdict

Two years on, the iPhone 15 Pro Max remains a compelling flagship. The 5x zoom is still excellent, the battery life is competitive, and the titanium build feels as premium as the day it launched. If you want the Pro Max experience at a lower price, this is it.
