## Design and Build: Tried and Tested

The iPhone 14 retains the familiar flat-edged design introduced with the iPhone 12. The aluminium frame and glass back are solid and durable, and the Ceramic Shield front remains one of the toughest phone screens on the market. It's IP68 rated, so splashes and dunks are no concern.

While it lacks the Dynamic Island of the 15 series, the notch is still functional — Face ID works reliably, and the familiarity is comforting. The Lightning port reminds you this is the last generation before USB-C, but it still charges and syncs perfectly.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The iPhone 14 is powered by the A15 Bionic chip — the same processor that powered the iPhone 13 Pro. It remains capable for all everyday tasks, social media, and light gaming.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Camera:** 12MP dual camera system (main + ultra-wide). The main sensor is larger than the iPhone 13's, with better low-light performance and improved stabilisation.
- **Display:** 6.1-inch OLED with 60Hz. Bright and colour-accurate.
- **Battery:** Up to 20 hours of video playback. A full day is achievable with moderate use.
- **Safety:** Crash Detection and Satellite SOS — potentially life-saving features.

## Who Is the iPhone 14 Actually For?

The iPhone 14 is for the budget-conscious buyer who prioritises safety features and reliability. If you're upgrading from an iPhone 11 or older, the improvements in camera, performance, and display will feel significant.

## The Verdict

In 2026, the iPhone 14 is a budget-friendly entry point into the Apple ecosystem. It's not the fastest or the most feature-rich, but it does everything most people need and does it reliably. The A15 chip still holds up, the camera takes good photos, and you get Crash Detection and Satellite SOS.
