## Design and Build: Battery Beast

The Galaxy M51 was the ultimate battery phone, featuring a massive 7,000mAh battery that was one of the largest ever put in a mainstream smartphone. Despite the huge battery, Samsung managed to keep the phone reasonably slim at 8.5mm and 213g — impressive given the capacity.

The Infinity-O display with hole-punch camera gave it a modern, flagship-inspired look. The phone felt substantial in hand but not uncomfortably heavy.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy M51 was defined by its extraordinary battery capacity.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Battery:** 7,000mAh with 25W fast charging. The phone could easily last three to four days of moderate use — even heavy users would struggle to drain it in a single day.
- **Display:** 6.7-inch Super AMOLED Plus, FHD+. The large AMOLED display was excellent for media consumption.
- **Camera:** 64MP main + 12MP ultra-wide + 5MP macro + 5MP depth. The 64MP main sensor captured detailed photos.
- **Processor:** Snapdragon 730G.
- **Storage:** 128GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy M51 was the phone for users who never wanted to worry about battery life. The 7,000mAh capacity was extraordinary, and the Super AMOLED display and 64MP camera made it a well-rounded package. In its class, nothing came close to its endurance.
