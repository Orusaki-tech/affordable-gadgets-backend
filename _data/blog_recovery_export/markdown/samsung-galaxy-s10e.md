## Design and Build: Compact Perfection

The Galaxy S10e was the phone that compact phone enthusiasts loved — a flat 5.8-inch display (no curved edges), a side-mounted fingerprint sensor that was fast and reliable, and a more affordable price than its siblings. The flat screen meant easy screen protector application and no accidental touches.

The size was perfect for one-handed use, weighing just 150g and fitting comfortably in any pocket. The headphone jack was retained, making it a favourite among audiophiles with wired IEMs.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy S10e was the most affordable S10 but still packed the same flagship processor.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 5.8-inch FHD+ Dynamic AMOLED, flat display. The flat screen is a pro, not a con.
- **Camera:** 12MP main + 16MP ultra-wide. No telephoto lens, but the main and ultra-wide produced great photos.
- **Fingerprint Sensor:** Side-mounted (power button) — fast and natural.
- **Battery:** 3,100mAh — small by today's standards. Expect a charge by midday.
- **Processor:** Snapdragon 855 / Exynos 9820.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy S10e was the compact king of its era. In 2026, it's a tiny, pocketable phone with a great display. Battery life is the main compromise. If you want a small phone for basic use, this is a strong budget option.
