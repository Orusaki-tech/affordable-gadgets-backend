## Design and Build: The USB-C Revolution

The iPhone 15 marked a historic shift for Apple — the transition from Lightning to USB-C. Two years later, that decision has aged beautifully. You can charge your iPhone, MacBook, iPad, and AirPods with the same cable, and the faster transfer speeds make backing up photos and videos significantly quicker.

The design introduced the Dynamic Island (previously Pro-only), giving the base iPhone a modern look. The aluminium frame with colour-infused glass back feels premium, and the phone remains IP68 water-resistant.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The iPhone 15 is powered by the A16 Bionic chip — still a very capable processor that handles iOS 20 and all current apps with ease.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Camera:** 48MP main camera with 2x optical-quality zoom. Photos are detailed with natural colours, and Portrait mode now works without switching to a dedicated mode.
- **Display:** 6.1-inch OLED with 60Hz. The Dynamic Island makes notifications and live activities more useful.
- **Battery:** Up to 20 hours of video playback. Still enough for a full day of moderate use.
- **USB-C:** Universal charging and faster data transfers.

## Who Is the iPhone 15 Actually For?

The iPhone 15 is for budget-conscious buyers who want a modern iPhone with USB-C and the Dynamic Island. It's an excellent upgrade from an iPhone 12 or older.

## The Verdict

In 2026, the iPhone 15 remains a capable and well-rounded phone. The A16 chip still delivers smooth performance, the 48MP camera takes great photos, and USB-C future-proofs your charging setup.
