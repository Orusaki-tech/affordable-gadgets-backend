## Design and Build: Refined and Durable

The Galaxy A34 built on its predecessor with a more modern design, featuring a flat Super AMOLED display with a U-shaped notch and minimal bezels. The Key Island button housing on the right edge made the controls easy to find by touch.

The phone retained IP67 water and dust resistance — a standout feature at this price. The plastic back had a subtle gradient finish that looked surprisingly premium, and the overall build quality was excellent.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy A34 upgraded to a 120Hz Super AMOLED display, making it one of the smoothest phones in its segment.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 6.6-inch Super AMOLED, 120Hz, 1,000 nits. The 120Hz refresh rate delivered exceptionally smooth scrolling and animations, matching the experience of much more expensive phones.
- **Camera:** 48MP main with OIS + 8MP ultra-wide + 5MP macro + 2MP depth. OIS ensured stable video and sharper low-light photos.
- **Battery:** 5,000mAh with 25W fast charging. All-day battery life with fast top-ups.
- **IP Rating:** IP67 — dust and water resistant.
- **Processor:** MediaTek Dimensity 1080 — a capable mid-range chip.
- **Storage:** 128GB/256GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy A34 was one of the best mid-range phones of its year. The 120Hz Super AMOLED display, IP67 water resistance, OIS camera, and large battery made it a compelling all-rounder that punched well above its price.
