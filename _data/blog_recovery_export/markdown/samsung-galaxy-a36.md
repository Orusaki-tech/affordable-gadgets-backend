## Design and Build: Sleek and Capable

The Galaxy A36 follows the established A-series formula with a flat Super AMOLED display, minimal bezels, and a clean back panel. The Key Island design makes the side-mounted buttons and fingerprint sensor easy to locate. The phone's build quality is excellent, with Gorilla Glass protection and an IP67 rating.

Samsung has included stereo speakers tuned by AKG, making this a great device for media consumption. The phone feels premium despite its plastic frame, and the available colours add personality.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy A36 delivers a well-rounded mid-range experience.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 6.6-inch Super AMOLED, 120Hz, 1,000 nits. Bright, smooth, and vibrant.
- **Camera:** 50MP main with OIS + 8MP ultra-wide + 5MP macro + 2MP depth. OIS ensures stable video and sharp photos.
- **Battery:** 5,000mAh with 25W fast charging.
- **IP Rating:** IP67 water and dust resistance.
- **Processor:** Exynos 1380.
- **Storage:** 128GB/256GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy A36 is another solid entry in Samsung's A-series lineup. It delivers a premium-feeling experience with a smooth display, capable camera, and durable build — all at a mid-range price.
