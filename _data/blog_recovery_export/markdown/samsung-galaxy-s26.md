## Overview

The Galaxy S26+ takes everything great about the S26 and adds a larger 6.7-inch display and a bigger battery. It's the sweet spot between the compact S26 and the Ultra's camera powerhouse.

## Key Features

- **Display:** 6.7-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X, 120Hz.
- **Battery:** 5,200mAh with 45W fast charging.
- **Camera:** 50MP + 12MP ultra-wide + 10MP telephoto (3x).
- **Chip:** Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 4.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy S26+ is the Goldilocks option — big enough for media consumption, powerful enough for anything, without the Ultra price premium.
