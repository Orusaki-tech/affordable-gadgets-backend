## Design and Build: Familiar Yet Refined

The AirPods Pro 3 continues Apple's iconic design with the short stem and silicone ear tips for a secure fit. The force sensor on the stem provides tactile control for playback and noise cancellation. The charging case is slightly wider but still pocket-friendly.

Apple has improved the fit with new ear tip sizes, and the adaptive transparency mode automatically adjusts to loud noises, making them more comfortable for extended wear.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The AirPods Pro 3 are powered by Apple's latest H-series chip, delivering significant improvements in audio processing and efficiency.

Here is a quick look at the standout features:

- **Active Noise Cancellation:** Significantly improved ANC that rivals the best in the industry. The adaptive transparency mode lets in ambient sound while automatically reducing loud noises.
- **Sound Quality:** Custom driver and amplifier with support for Lossless Audio when paired with Apple Vision Pro.
- **Audio Features:** Adaptive EQ, Personalized Spatial Audio with dynamic head tracking, and conversation awareness.
- **Battery:** Up to 6 hours of listening time (33 hours with case). USB-C charging and MagSafe compatible.
- **Chip:** Apple H3 chip for improved audio processing and connectivity.

## The Verdict

The AirPods Pro 3 is the best pair of wireless earbuds for Apple users. The improved ANC, excellent sound quality, and deep ecosystem integration make them the default choice for iPhone users.
