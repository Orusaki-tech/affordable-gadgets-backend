## Design and Build: Premium Touches at a Budget Price

The Galaxy A22 was a standout mid-range phone that offered features typically reserved for more expensive devices. The Super AMOLED display with 90Hz refresh rate was the headline feature, and the phone's design followed Samsung's refined aesthetic with a slim profile and clean camera housing.

The plastic back had a subtle textured finish that resisted fingerprints, and the phone felt solid despite its lightweight 186g construction. Samsung retained the 3.5mm headphone jack and included a side-mounted fingerprint sensor.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy A22 offered a combination of features that was rare in its price segment.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 6.4-inch Super AMOLED, 90Hz. The 90Hz refresh rate made scrolling and animations noticeably smoother than standard 60Hz panels.
- **Camera:** 48MP main with OIS + 8MP ultra-wide + 2MP macro + 2MP depth. Optical Image Stabilisation (OIS) on the main camera was a real highlight — it significantly improved low-light photography and video stability.
- **Battery:** 5,000mAh with 15W charging. Excellent battery life with the efficient AMOLED display.
- **Processor:** MediaTek Helio G80, capable of handling light gaming.
- **Storage:** 64GB/128GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy A22 was one of the best value phones Samsung ever made. The combination of a 90Hz Super AMOLED display and OIS-equipped camera at this price point was exceptional. In 2026, it remains a capable and affordable daily driver.
