## Design and Build: The People's Phone

The Galaxy A51 was the best-selling Android phone globally in 2020 — and for good reason. It offered a stunning Super AMOLED display, versatile quad-camera system, and Samsung's refined One UI at a price that appealed to millions. The Infinity-O display with a hole-punch camera gave it a flagship-inspired look.

The back panel featured a distinctive L-shaped camera housing (a design choice that became an A-series hallmark), and the prism-patterned back caught the light beautifully. At 187g, it felt substantial without being heavy.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy A51 was powered by an Exynos 9611 processor with 4GB or 6GB of RAM.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 6.5-inch Super AMOLED, FHD+. The Infinity-O display offered an immersive viewing experience with vibrant colours and deep blacks.
- **Camera:** 48MP main + 12MP ultra-wide + 5MP macro + 5MP depth. The quad-camera setup offered exceptional versatility, including dedicated macro photography.
- **Battery:** 4,000mAh with 15W fast charging. Reliable battery life for a full day of use.
- **In-Display Fingerprint:** Optical under-display sensor.
- **Storage:** 64GB/128GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy A51 earned its place as the best-selling Android phone of its year by delivering an exceptional all-round experience at an accessible price. The Super AMOLED display, quad cameras, and modern design made it a compelling choice for millions.
