## Overview

The iPhone 7 was a landmark iPhone — it removed the headphone jack, introduced IP67 water resistance, and the jet black finish became an instant classic. The A10 Fusion chip and 12MP camera made it a capable device for its era.

## Key Features

- **Display:** 4.7-inch Retina HD display.
- **Camera:** 12MP main camera with OIS.
- **Chip:** A10 Fusion — very dated, basic apps only.
- **Home Button:** Solid-state with Taptic Engine feedback.

## The Verdict

In 2026, the iPhone 7 is an ultra-budget option or a secondary phone for calls and basic messaging. It no longer supports the latest iOS versions, so app compatibility is limited.
