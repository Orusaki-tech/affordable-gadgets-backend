## Design and Build: Refined and Capable

The Galaxy A57 sits at the top of Samsung's A-series lineup, offering a near-flagship experience with a few strategic compromises to keep the price accessible. The flat Super AMOLED display, aluminium frame, and Gorilla Glass Victus+ protection make it feel every bit as premium as more expensive phones.

The Key Island design returns, and the phone features IP67 water resistance and stereo speakers. The back panel camera housing is clean and modern.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy A57 delivers strong performance for all everyday tasks.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 6.6-inch Super AMOLED, 120Hz, 1,000 nits.
- **Camera:** 50MP main with OIS + 12MP ultra-wide + 5MP macro + 5MP depth.
- **Battery:** 5,000mAh with 45W fast charging.
- **Build:** Aluminium frame and Gorilla Glass Victus+.
- **IP Rating:** IP67 water and dust resistance.
- **Processor:** Exynos 1480.
- **Storage:** 128GB/256GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy A57 is the phone to get if you want a premium experience without paying flagship prices. The build quality, display, and fast charging are standout features.
