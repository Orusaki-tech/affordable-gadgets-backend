## Design and Build: Budget-Friendly, Not Budget-Feeling

The Galaxy A05 is an entry-level smartphone that punches above its weight in design. Samsung has given it the same Infinity-V display and sleek profile as its more expensive siblings, with a textured back that resists fingerprints and provides a secure grip. The plastic construction keeps it lightweight at just 195g.

Despite the low price point, the A05 feels solid in the hand. The 3.5mm headphone jack and USB-C port are both present, and the side-mounted fingerprint sensor (integrated into the power button) is a welcome addition at this price level.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy A05 is powered by a MediaTek Helio G85 processor paired with 4GB of RAM and 64GB or 128GB of storage (expandable via microSD).

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 6.7-inch HD+ PLS LCD with 60Hz refresh rate. The large screen is great for media consumption, though the resolution caps out at 720p+.
- **Camera:** 50MP main + 2MP depth sensor. The 50MP main camera captures surprisingly detailed photos in good lighting, with Samsung's scene optimiser enhancing colours automatically.
- **Battery:** 5,000mAh with 15W charging. The large battery delivers excellent endurance — expect two days of moderate use.
- **Software:** One UI 5.x based on Android 13, with Samsung's promise of security updates.

## Who Is the Galaxy A05 Actually For?

The Galaxy A05 is for first-time smartphone buyers, students, or anyone who needs a reliable device for calls, messaging, social media, and light media consumption. It's also an excellent backup phone or a secondary device for work.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy A05 delivers the core Samsung experience at a very accessible price point. The 50MP camera, 5,000mAh battery, and large display make it a compelling option in the budget segment. It won't handle gaming or heavy multitasking, but for everyday essentials, it delivers everything you need.
