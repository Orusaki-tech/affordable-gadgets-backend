## Design and Build: The Big-Screen Champion

The Galaxy A70 was designed for media lovers who wanted a large, immersive display without paying flagship prices. The 6.7-inch Super AMOLED Infinity-U display was one of the largest available on a mid-range phone at launch, making it perfect for watching videos, gaming, and multitasking.

The phone featured a glossy back with a subtle prism pattern that caught the light, and the triple-camera housing was neatly integrated. At 187g, it was reasonably light for its size.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy A70 was powered by a Snapdragon 675 processor, which handled everyday tasks and gaming well.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 6.7-inch Super AMOLED, FHD+. The large AMOLED display delivered vibrant colours and deep blacks, perfect for Netflix and YouTube.
- **Camera:** 32MP main + 8MP ultra-wide + 5MP depth. The 32MP front camera was also impressive for selfies.
- **Battery:** 4,500mAh with 25W fast charging — faster than most mid-range phones at launch.
- **In-Display Fingerprint:** Optical under-display sensor.
- **Storage:** 128GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy A70 was a compelling choice for anyone who prioritised display size and battery life. The large Super AMOLED screen and fast charging made it a media powerhouse at a mid-range price.
