## Design and Build: Evolution of the Budget Galaxy

The Galaxy A16 continues Samsung's tradition of delivering premium-feeling budget phones. The Key Island design with its raised button housing returns, and the flat Super AMOLED display dominates the front with minimal bezels. The camera island on the back is clean and modern.

The phone feels solid in hand, with good weight distribution despite the plastic construction. The 3.5mm headphone jack and USB-C port are present, along with a dedicated microSD slot.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy A16 refines the formula established by the A15 with a more capable processor.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 6.7-inch Super AMOLED, 90Hz, 800 nits. The larger display is immersive for media consumption, with vibrant colours and deep blacks.
- **Camera:** 50MP main + 5MP ultra-wide + 2MP macro. Reliable performance for social media and casual photography.
- **Battery:** 5,000mAh with 25W charging. The large battery delivers consistent all-day battery life.
- **Processor:** MediaTek Dimensity 6300 — a capable mid-range chip for smooth everyday performance.
- **Software:** One UI 6 with Android 14, with Samsung's commitment to security updates.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy A16 is a well-rounded budget smartphone that doesn't compromise on the most important features. The Super AMOLED display, 90Hz refresh rate, and 5,000mAh battery make it a compelling choice for anyone seeking an affordable daily driver.
