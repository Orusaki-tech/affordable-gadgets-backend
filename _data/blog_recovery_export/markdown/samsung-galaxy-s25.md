## Overview

The Galaxy S25+ sits between the compact S25 and the Ultra, offering a 6.7-inch display, larger battery, and faster 45W charging while keeping the same core camera system.

## Key Features

- **Display:** 6.7-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X, 120Hz.
- **Battery:** 5,000mAh with 45W fast charging.
- **Camera:** 50MP + 12MP ultra-wide + 10MP telephoto (3x).
- **Chip:** Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 3.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy S25+ is the smart choice for anyone who wants a large display and all-day battery without the Ultra price.
