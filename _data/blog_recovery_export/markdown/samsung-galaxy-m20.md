## Design and Build: The Original M-Series

The Galaxy M20 was Samsung's first entry in the M-series lineup, designed specifically for the online-focused buyer who wanted maximum battery life. The Infinity-V display with waterdrop notch and glossy plastic back gave it a modern look that was competitive with other budget phones of its era.

At 186g, the phone was reasonably light considering its 5,000mAh battery. Samsung included both a 3.5mm headphone jack and a USB-C port.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy M20 set the template for the M-series: big battery, good display, competitive price.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Battery:** 5,000mAh with 15W fast charging. The large battery was the phone's defining feature, delivering excellent endurance.
- **Display:** 6.3-inch FHD+ PLS LCD, Infinity-V. The FHD+ resolution was sharper than most budget phones at launch.
- **Camera:** 13MP main + 5MP ultra-wide. The dual-camera setup offered versatility with wide-angle photography.
- **Processor:** Exynos 7904.
- **Storage:** 32GB/64GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy M20 was a trailblazer that established the M-series formula of big batteries at budget prices. It proved that you didn't need to spend a lot to get exceptional battery life.
