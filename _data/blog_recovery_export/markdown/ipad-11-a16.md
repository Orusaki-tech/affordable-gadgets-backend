## Design and Build: The Classic iPad Formula

The iPad 11 (A16) continues Apple's winning formula — a 10.9-inch Liquid Retina display, aluminium unibody construction, and the simplicity of iPadOS. At just over a pound, it's lightweight enough to carry everywhere.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The A16 Bionic chip brings desktop-class performance to the budget iPad. It handles multitasking, creative apps, and even light video editing with ease.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 10.9-inch Liquid Retina display with True Tone.
- **Chip:** A16 Bionic — the same chip found in the iPhone 15.
- **Camera:** 12MP wide camera with Centre Stage for video calls.
- **Connectivity:** USB-C, Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.3.
- **Accessories:** Supports Apple Pencil (1st gen) and Magic Keyboard Folio.

## Who Is It For?

The iPad 11 is for students, casual users, and anyone who wants a versatile tablet for browsing, streaming, note-taking, and light creative work.
