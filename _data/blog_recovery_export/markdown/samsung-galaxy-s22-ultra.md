## Design and Build: The Note Spirit Lives On

The Galaxy S22 Ultra was a landmark phone that merged the Galaxy Note and S series into a single device. The boxy design with flat top and bottom edges, the built-in S Pen silo, and the massive camera housing were unmistakably Note-inspired.

The phone featured a premium glass-and-metal construction with Gorilla Glass Victus+ and a matte back finish that resisted fingerprints. At 228g, it was hefty but felt reassuringly solid.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy S22 Ultra was a photography and productivity powerhouse.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **S Pen:** Built-in silo with Bluetooth Air Actions and 2.8ms latency.
- **Camera:** 108MP main + 12MP ultra-wide + 10MP telephoto (3x) + 10MP telephoto (10x). The 10x optical zoom was exceptional.
- **Display:** 6.8-inch QHD+ Dynamic AMOLED 2X, 120Hz. Samsung's best display technology.
- **Battery:** 5,000mAh with 45W charging. Excellent battery life.
- **Chipset:** Snapdragon 8 Gen 1 / Exynos 2200.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy S22 Ultra was the spiritual successor to the Note, offering S Pen productivity, 10x optical zoom, and a stunning display. In 2026, it remains a capable productivity flagship at a reduced price.
