## Design and Build: A Step Up in Style

The Galaxy A12 was a popular budget smartphone that introduced Samsung's modern design language to the entry-level segment. The geometric patterned back panel gave it a distinctive look that stood out from the plain plastic backs of competitors. The 8.9mm thickness is reasonable, though the 205g weight is noticeable.

The side-mounted fingerprint sensor (integrated with the power button) was a significant upgrade from rear-mounted sensors, making it more natural to unlock the phone when picking it up.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy A12 is powered by a MediaTek Helio P35 processor, which handles basic tasks and light gaming reasonably well.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Camera:** 48MP main + 5MP ultra-wide + 2MP macro + 2MP depth. The quad-camera setup was impressive for its price segment, and the 48MP main sensor captures detailed shots in good lighting.
- **Display:** 6.5-inch HD+ PLS LCD, 60Hz. The Infinity-V display offers good colour reproduction and adequate brightness for indoor use.
- **Battery:** 5,000mAh with 15W charging. Excellent battery life that comfortably lasts two days with moderate use.
- **Storage:** 32GB/64GB/128GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy A12 was one of the best-selling budget Android phones for good reason: it offered a 48MP quad camera, a massive 5,000mAh battery, and Samsung's trusted One UI at a very accessible price. In 2026, it remains a capable budget option for basic smartphone needs.
