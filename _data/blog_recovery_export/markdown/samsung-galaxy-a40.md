## Design and Build: Small Phone, Big Heart

The Galaxy A40 was designed for users who wanted a compact phone without breaking the bank. At just 5.9 inches, it was one of the smaller Android phones of its era, making it perfect for one-handed use. The Infinity-U display with a small waterdrop notch gave it a modern look.

The plastic back featured a glossy finish with a subtle pattern that caught the light. At just 140g, it was incredibly lightweight and pocketable. Samsung retained the 3.5mm headphone jack.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy A40 offered a Super AMOLED display at a very accessible price.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 5.9-inch Super AMOLED, FHD+. The AMOLED panel delivered deep blacks and vibrant colours in a compact size.
- **Camera:** 16MP main + 5MP ultra-wide. The versatile dual-camera setup offered both standard and wide-angle photography.
- **Battery:** 3,100mAh with 15W charging. Suitable for light to moderate use.
- **Storage:** 64GB, expandable via microSD.
- **Processor:** Exynos 7904.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy A40 was a niche but beloved device for those who wanted a compact Super AMOLED experience on a budget. In 2026, it's an older device suitable for basic use or as a secondary phone.
