## Design and Build: Familiar Galaxy DNA

The Galaxy A31 adopted the Infinity-U display design with a waterdrop notch, following the same aesthetic as its predecessor. The back panel featured a distinctive L-shaped camera housing that held the four lenses — a design choice that made it instantly recognisable.

The phone's plastic construction kept it light at 185g, making it comfortable for extended use. The rear-mounted fingerprint sensor was reliable, and Samsung retained the 3.5mm headphone jack.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy A31 was one of the few budget phones to offer a quad-camera system, giving users versatility rarely seen at this price.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Camera:** 48MP main + 8MP ultra-wide + 5MP macro + 5MP depth. The quad-camera setup offered flexibility for different shooting scenarios, with the 48MP main sensor delivering detailed daylight photos.
- **Display:** 6.4-inch Super AMOLED, FHD+. The AMOLED panel produced vibrant colours and deep blacks.
- **Battery:** 5,000mAh with 15W charging. A massive battery for its time, delivering excellent endurance.
- **Processor:** MediaTek Helio P65.
- **Storage:** 64GB/128GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy A31 offered incredible camera versatility and display quality for its price. The quad-camera system and Super AMOLED panel made it a strong contender in the budget segment. In 2026, it remains a capable option for basic smartphone needs.
