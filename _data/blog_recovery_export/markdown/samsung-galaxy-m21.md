## Design and Build: Best of Both Worlds

The Galaxy M21 combined two things that budget buyers loved: a Super AMOLED display and a massive battery. This made it one of the most compelling budget phones of its generation. The Infinity-U display with waterdrop notch delivered the vibrant colours and deep blacks that Samsung's AMOLED panels are famous for.

The back panel had a clean design with a rectangular camera housing. The phone was comfortable to hold despite its 188g weight.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy M21 offered a unique combination of premium display and exceptional battery life.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 6.4-inch Super AMOLED, FHD+. The AMOLED panel was the standout feature, delivering stunning colour reproduction and contrast.
- **Battery:** 6,000mAh with 15W charging. The massive battery paired with the efficient AMOLED display delivered exceptional endurance.
- **Camera:** 48MP main + 8MP ultra-wide + 5MP depth.
- **Processor:** Exynos 9611.
- **Storage:** 64GB/128GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy M21 was one of the best budget phones of its era. The combination of a Super AMOLED display and 6,000mAh battery was unique and compelling. For media consumption and battery life, it was hard to beat.
