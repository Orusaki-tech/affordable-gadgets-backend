## Design and Build: Camera and Battery King

The Galaxy M31 was the successor to the M30s with a significant camera upgrade. The 64MP main camera was a major step up, capable of capturing highly detailed images. The Infinity-U display with waterdrop notch and the textured back panel made it distinctive.

The phone was well-built for its price segment, with the rear-mounted fingerprint sensor proving fast and reliable. Samsung retained the 3.5mm headphone jack.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy M31 offered a compelling combination of a high-resolution camera and exceptional battery life.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Camera:** 64MP main + 8MP ultra-wide + 5MP macro + 5MP depth. The 64MP sensor captured incredibly detailed photos with Samsung's vibrant colour processing.
- **Battery:** 6,000mAh with 15W charging. Exceptional endurance that could last days.
- **Display:** 6.4-inch Super AMOLED, FHD+. Beautiful colours and contrast.
- **Processor:** Exynos 9611.
- **Storage:** 64GB/128GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy M31 was a budget phone that excelled in two key areas: camera resolution and battery life. The 64MP camera and 6,000mAh battery made it a compelling choice for photography enthusiasts on a budget.
