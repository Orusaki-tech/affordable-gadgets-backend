## Design and Build: Small Yet Mighty

The Galaxy A41 improved on the A40 with a larger display while maintaining a compact footprint. The Infinity-U display with waterdrop notch was bright and colourful, and the back panel featured a distinctive rectangular camera housing that housed the triple-camera system.

The phone was lightweight at just 152g and comfortable to use one-handed. Samsung retained the 3.5mm headphone jack and included a USB-C port.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy A41 upgraded the camera system compared to the A40.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 6.1-inch Super AMOLED, FHD+. The AMOLED display delivered excellent colour reproduction and deep blacks.
- **Camera:** 48MP main + 8MP ultra-wide + 5MP depth. The 48MP main sensor captured detailed photos with good dynamic range.
- **Battery:** 3,500mAh with 15W charging.
- **Storage:** 64GB, expandable via microSD.
- **Processor:** MediaTek Helio P65.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy A41 was a solid compact mid-range phone that offered a 48MP camera and beautiful AMOLED display in a pocket-friendly size. In 2026, it's an older device suitable for basic everyday use.
