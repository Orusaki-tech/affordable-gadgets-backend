## Design and Build: Display Meets Endurance

The Galaxy M32 brought a 90Hz Super AMOLED display to the budget M-series — a significant upgrade that made everyday use feel smoother and more premium. The Infinity-U display with waterdrop notch and the textured back panel with a large camera housing gave it a distinctive look.

The phone felt solid and well-balanced despite its 196g weight. The side-mounted fingerprint sensor was fast, and Samsung retained the 3.5mm headphone jack.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy M32 offered a standout display at its price point.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 6.4-inch Super AMOLED, 90Hz, FHD+. The 90Hz refresh rate made scrolling and animations noticeably smoother than standard 60Hz panels.
- **Battery:** 6,000mAh with 25W fast charging. Exceptional endurance with faster charging than previous M-series models.
- **Camera:** 64MP main + 8MP ultra-wide + 2MP macro + 2MP depth.
- **Processor:** MediaTek Helio G80.
- **Storage:** 64GB/128GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy M32 was one of the best budget phones for display quality and battery life. The 90Hz Super AMOLED panel was a rare find at this price, and the 6,000mAh battery ensured it kept running.
