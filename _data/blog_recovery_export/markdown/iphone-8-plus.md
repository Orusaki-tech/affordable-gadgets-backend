## Overview

The iPhone 8 Plus combined the classic home button design with the large 5.5-inch display, dual cameras, and the A11 Bionic chip. The glass back enabled wireless charging, and Portrait mode with Portrait Lighting gave photographers more creative control.

## Key Features

- **Camera:** 12MP dual cameras with Portrait mode and Portrait Lighting.
- **Display:** 5.5-inch Retina HD display with True Tone.
- **Chip:** A11 Bionic.
- **Wireless Charging:** Qi-compatible.

## The Verdict

For those who miss the home button and want a large display at a very low price, the iPhone 8 Plus still works for basic use in 2026.
