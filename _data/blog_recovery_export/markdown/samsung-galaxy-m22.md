## Design and Build: Premium Features, Budget Price

The Galaxy M22 was the first M-series phone to feature a 90Hz Super AMOLED display and OIS camera — features rarely seen in this price segment. The Infinity-U display with waterdrop notch and clean back panel with a rectangular camera housing gave it a modern, premium appearance.

The phone was surprisingly light at 185g despite the large battery. Samsung retained the 3.5mm headphone jack.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy M22 offered a feature set that punched well above its price class.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 6.4-inch Super AMOLED, 90Hz. The 90Hz refresh rate and AMOLED quality made this one of the best displays in its price segment.
- **Camera:** 48MP main with OIS + 8MP ultra-wide + 2MP macro + 2MP depth. OIS on the main camera helped capture sharper photos and smoother video.
- **Battery:** 5,000mAh with 25W fast charging.
- **Processor:** MediaTek Helio G80.
- **Storage:** 64GB/128GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy M22 offered exceptional value with its 90Hz Super AMOLED display and OIS camera. It was one of the best budget phones for anyone who prioritised display quality and camera stability.
