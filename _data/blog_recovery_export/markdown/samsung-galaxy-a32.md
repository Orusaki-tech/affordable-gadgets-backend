## Design and Build: A Serious Upgrade

The Galaxy A32 was a significant step forward for Samsung's A-series, introducing a 90Hz Super AMOLED display and a 64MP main camera — features that were previously reserved for much more expensive phones. The design followed Samsung's refined aesthetic with a clean back panel and a rectangular camera housing.

The phone was available in a range of attractive colours, and the matte finish on the back resisted fingerprints. At 184g, it was reasonably lightweight.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy A32 offered a compelling combination of features that punch well above its price class.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 6.4-inch Super AMOLED, 90Hz, 800 nits. The 90Hz refresh rate made the phone feel incredibly responsive, with smooth scrolling and animations.
- **Camera:** 64MP main + 8MP ultra-wide + 5MP macro + 5MP depth. The 64MP sensor captured detailed images with vibrant Samsung-style colours.
- **Battery:** 5,000mAh with 15W charging. The large battery paired with the efficient AMOLED display delivered excellent endurance.
- **Processor:** MediaTek Helio G80.
- **Storage:** 64GB/128GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy A32 was one of the best value phones of its generation. The 90Hz Super AMOLED display made everyday use a pleasure, and the 64MP camera captured great photos. In 2026, it's still a very capable budget phone that offers a premium-feeling experience.
