## Design and Build: The Pinnacle of Smartphone Design

The iPhone 17 Pro Max is the largest and most ambitious iPhone Apple has ever built. The 6.9-inch OLED display is virtually bezel-free, wrapped in a polished titanium frame that feels both luxurious and durable. At 223g, it's heavy — but the weight distribution is excellent, and it feels reassuringly solid in the hand.

The rear camera system takes up a significant portion of the back, and for good reason: Apple has equipped the Pro Max with its most advanced camera hardware yet, including a periscope telephoto lens and the largest main sensor ever fitted to an iPhone.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The iPhone 17 Pro Max runs the A19 Pro chip, the most powerful mobile processor on the planet. It handles 8K video editing, AAA gaming, and complex AI workloads without breaking a sweat.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Camera:** 48MP main with a physically larger sensor, 48MP ultra-wide, 12MP periscope telephoto with 8x optical zoom. The main sensor captures 30% more light than the iPhone 16 Pro Max.
- **Display:** 6.9-inch LTPO OLED with 120Hz ProMotion, 2,200 nits peak brightness, and the thinnest bezels on any phone.
- **Battery:** Up to 36 hours of video playback — the longest battery life of any iPhone ever made.
- **Recording:** 8K 30fps ProRes video recording with Log colour profile and Apple ProRes RAW support for professional workflows.

## Who Is the iPhone 17 Pro Max Actually For?

The iPhone 17 Pro Max is for professionals and power users who demand the absolute best. If you're a filmmaker, photographer, or content creator who relies on your phone as a primary tool, the Pro Max's camera system, battery life, and performance are unmatched.

## The Verdict

The iPhone 17 Pro Max is the best smartphone money can buy. It has the largest display, the longest battery life, the most powerful chip, and the most versatile camera system in Apple's history. If you want the absolute best, this is it.
