## Design and Build: A True Flagship Experience

The Galaxy S10+ was the big brother of the S10 lineup, featuring a stunning 6.4-inch QHD+ Dynamic AMOLED display with curved edges that made the phone feel surprisingly manageable despite its size. The ceramic back option on higher-end models added a premium feel that still turns heads.

The dual front-facing cameras were accommodated in an oval hole-punch cutout — controversial at the time, but Samsung's implementation was clean and minimized screen obstruction.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy S10+ packed flagship specs of 2019 that are now showing their age.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 6.4-inch WQHD+ Dynamic AMOLED, HDR10+. The display quality remains excellent — deep blacks, vibrant colours, and excellent brightness.
- **Camera:** 12MP main + 16MP ultra-wide + 12MP telephoto (2x). The dual front cameras (10MP + 8MP depth sensor) enabled portrait selfies.
- **Battery:** 4,100mAh — the largest battery in the S10 series, offering decent endurance even today.
- **Headphone Jack:** Yes — with AKG-tuned earphones included.
- **Storage:** Up to 1TB (including microSD expansion).

## The Verdict

The Galaxy S10+ in 2026 is a budget-friendly phone with a gorgeous display. The processor won't handle demanding games or heavy multitasking, but for basic use and media consumption, the screen is still a pleasure to look at.
