## Design and Build: Modern Budget Design

The Galaxy M14 brought 5G connectivity to Samsung's budget M-series, making it an excellent choice for future-proofing. The Infinity-V display and clean back panel with a triple-camera housing gave it a modern look that didn't scream "budget."

The side-mounted fingerprint sensor was fast, and the phone felt solid despite the plastic construction. At 208g, the weight reflected the large battery inside.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy M14 offered 5G connectivity and impressive battery life at an entry-level price.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Battery:** 6,000mAh with 25W fast charging. The massive battery delivered exceptional endurance, easily lasting two days or more.
- **5G:** Sub-6GHz 5G support for faster data speeds.
- **Display:** 6.6-inch HD+ PLS LCD, 90Hz. The 90Hz refresh rate made scrolling smoother.
- **Camera:** 50MP main + 2MP macro + 2MP depth.
- **Processor:** Exynos 1330 (5G).
- **Storage:** 64GB/128GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy M14 was an excellent budget phone for anyone who wanted 5G connectivity and exceptional battery life. The 6,000mAh battery and 90Hz display made it a compelling choice in the entry-level segment.
