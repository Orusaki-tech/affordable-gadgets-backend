## Design and Build: Big iPhone, Smaller Price

The iPhone 14 Plus was Apple's answer to everyone who wanted a large-screen iPhone without paying Pro Max prices. The 6.7-inch OLED display delivers the same expansive canvas as the Pro Max, wrapped in the same flat-edged aluminium frame that defined the iPhone 12-14 era.

At 203g, it's lighter than the Pro Max, and the generous display size makes it ideal for media consumption, document reading, and navigation. The notch houses Face ID, and the IP68 rating provides peace of mind against water damage.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The A15 Bionic chip powers the iPhone 14 Plus with aplomb. Originally a flagship processor, it still handles iOS smoothly, multitasking without hesitation, and runs most games at high settings.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Battery:** Up to 26 hours of video playback — the biggest battery in the iPhone 14 series. The Plus model excels here, easily lasting two days for moderate users.
- **Display:** 6.7-inch Super Retina XDR OLED. Vibrant colours and deep blacks make for an excellent media consumption experience.
- **Camera:** 12MP dual cameras (main + ultra-wide). Night Mode, Deep Fusion, and Smart HDR 4 deliver consistently good photos.

## Who Is the iPhone 14 Plus Actually For?

The iPhone 14 Plus is for anyone who wants maximum battery life and screen size at a minimum price. If you watch a lot of video, need your phone to last through long days, or simply prefer larger displays, the 14 Plus delivers without the flagship markup.

## The Verdict

In 2026, the iPhone 14 Plus is one of the best value large-screen phones you can buy. The battery life is exceptional, the display is gorgeous, and the A15 chip still performs smoothly for all everyday tasks.
