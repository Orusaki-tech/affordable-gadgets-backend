## Design and Build: Samsung's Most Refined Design

The Galaxy S25+ continues Samsung's commitment to refined, premium design with a flat Dynamic AMOLED display, Armor Aluminum 2 frame, and Gorilla Glass Victus 2 protection. The matte back panel and clean camera housing give it an understated elegance.

The phone features a slightly thinner and lighter build than the S24+, improving ergonomics without sacrificing battery capacity. The IP68 water resistance rating provides peace of mind.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy S25+ is powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite for Galaxy — Qualcomm's latest flagship chip, delivering massive performance and efficiency gains.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Chipset:** Snapdragon 8 Elite for Galaxy — significant CPU and GPU improvements over the previous generation.
- **One UI 7:** Samsung's latest software with enhanced customization, improved multitasking, and deeper Galaxy AI integration.
- **Display:** 6.7-inch QHD+ Dynamic AMOLED 2X, 120Hz. Bright, sharp, and smooth.
- **Camera:** 50MP main + 12MP ultra-wide + 10MP telephoto (3x). AI-enhanced image processing delivers better results in all conditions.
- **Battery:** 4,900mAh with 45W charging.
- **Galaxy AI:** Enhanced AI features including AI Select, Drawing Assist, and improved Live Translate.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy S25+ is the most refined mid-sized flagship Samsung makes. The Snapdragon 8 Elite chip, One UI 7, and Galaxy AI features make it a powerful and intelligent daily driver.
