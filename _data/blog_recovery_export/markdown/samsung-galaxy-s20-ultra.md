## Design and Build: Bold and Unapologetic

The Galaxy S20 Ultra was Samsung's statement phone for 2020 — a massive device with an even more massive camera bump that housed the company's most ambitious camera system yet. The glass sandwich design with a metal frame felt undeniably premium, and the 6.9-inch display made it one of the largest phones of its era.

The sheer size (220g) made it a two-handed device through and through, but the curved display edges made it feel slightly narrower than you'd expect.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy S20 Ultra was the first phone to feature a 108MP camera sensor and 100x Space Zoom. While the zoom quality at 100x was more marketing than practical, the 4x optical and up to 10x hybrid zoom delivered genuinely impressive results.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Camera:** 108MP main + 48MP telephoto (4x optical, 10x hybrid) + 12MP ultra-wide. The huge 108MP sensor captured incredible detail in good light, and the 48MP telephoto sensor was larger than most phone's main cameras.
- **Display:** 6.9-inch WQHD+ Dynamic AMOLED 2X, 120Hz. The 120Hz mode only worked at FHD+ resolution, but the display quality was stunning.
- **Battery:** 5,000mAh with 45W charging (charger sold separately) and 15W wireless. Exceptional battery life.
- **Chip:** Snapdragon 865 / Exynos 990 — showing its age but still handles basic tasks.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy S20 Ultra was ahead of its time, introducing camera specs that became standard years later. In 2026, it's an affordable way to experience a huge display and a versatile camera system. The chip is dated, but for photography enthusiasts on a budget, the camera hardware still delivers.
