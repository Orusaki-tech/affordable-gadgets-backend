## Design and Build: Where Modern iPhones Began

The iPhone 12 marked Apple's return to flat edges, a design language that continues today. The aluminium frame and glass back feel solid, and the Ceramic Shield front offered 4x better drop protection than the iPhone 11.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The A14 Bionic chip still handles everyday tasks, social media, and light gaming. It was Apple's first 5nm processor.

- **Display:** 6.1-inch Super Retina XDR OLED — Apple finally brought OLED to the standard iPhone.
- **Camera:** 12MP dual cameras with Night Mode on both lenses.
- **MagSafe:** The iPhone 12 introduced MagSafe for easy accessory attachment and faster wireless charging.
- **5G:** First iPhone generation with 5G support.

## The Verdict

The iPhone 12 was a landmark design that defined the modern iPhone look. In 2026, it's a budget option that still takes good photos and runs iOS reliably. The A14 chip is getting long in the tooth, but for basic use, it still works.
