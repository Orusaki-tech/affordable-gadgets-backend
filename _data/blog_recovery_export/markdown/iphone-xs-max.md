## Overview

The iPhone XS Max was Apple's first iPhone with a large OLED display — 6.5 inches of Super Retina OLED. It combined the premium stainless steel build with the biggest screen Apple had ever put in a phone at that time.

## Key Features

- **Display:** 6.5-inch Super Retina OLED — stunning for its era.
- **Camera:** 12MP dual cameras with Smart HDR.
- **Chip:** A12 Bionic.
- **Battery:** Up to 15 hours of video playback.
- **Build:** Stainless steel frame with IP68 water resistance.

## The Verdict

In 2026, the iPhone XS Max offers a large OLED display and premium build at a very affordable price. It's suitable for media consumption and basic use.
