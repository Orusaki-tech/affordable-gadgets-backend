## Design and Build: Ultra-Light Without the Fragile Feel

At just 159 grams, the Sony Xperia 10 V is officially the world's lightest 5G smartphone. Pick it up and the first thing you notice is how effortlessly it sits in the hand — no wrist fatigue, no awkward balancing act. Yet Sony hasn't sacrificed build quality. The matte plastic back is surprisingly premium, resists fingerprints, and won't shatter if you drop it without a case.

The 6.1-inch OLED display is protected by Gorilla Glass Victus, and the phone carries an IP68 rating, meaning it survives dust and a dip in water. For a mid-range device, that's an exceptional level of durability.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Xperia 10 V strikes an impressive balance between weight, battery, and everyday performance. It's powered by the Snapdragon 695 chipset with 6GB of RAM — not a gaming powerhouse, but more than adequate for social media, streaming, navigation, and light multitasking.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Battery:** 5,000mAh — enormous for a 159g phone. Expect approximately 34 hours of video playback on a single charge. This is the phone's killer feature.
- **Display:** 6.1-inch FHD+ OLED with Triluminos display technology. Sony's expertise in screens (inherited from their TV division) means rich colours and deep blacks.
- **Camera:** Triple rear cameras — 48MP wide, 8MP ultra-wide, and 8MP telephoto. Sony's camera app includes the same manual controls found on the flagship Xperia 1 series.
- **Audio:** Dual front-facing stereo speakers with Sony's audio processing. The 3.5mm headphone jack is present with High-Resolution Audio support.

## Who Is the Xperia 10 V Actually For?

The Xperia 10 V is for anyone who prioritises battery life and portability above all else. If you are a frequent traveller, a student walking between classes, or someone who simply hates carrying a charger, this phone will easily get you through two days of moderate use.

It's also an excellent choice for audiophiles on a budget, thanks to the 3.5mm jack and Hi-Res Audio certification — a rare combination in 2026.

If you need raw gaming performance or the absolute best camera, the Galaxy A series or Pixel 7A may offer better value. But for sheer endurance and pocketability, the Xperia 10 V is unmatched.

## The Verdict

Sony has delivered something genuinely unique: a lightweight, water-resistant 5G phone that never leaves you scrambling for a charger. The Xperia 10 V isn't the flashiest phone on the shelf, but it solves the most common smartphone problem — dead battery — better than almost anything in its class.
