## Design and Build: The Contour-Cut Revolution

The Galaxy S21 marked a significant design departure for Samsung. The contour-cut camera housing that flows seamlessly into the aluminium frame was instantly iconic and has influenced Samsung's design language ever since. The plastic back (Glasstic) was a controversial choice, but it kept the phone lightweight at 169g and made it more durable against drops.

The flat 6.2-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X display was a welcome return after the curved displays of previous generations, making screen protectors easy to apply and reducing accidental touches.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy S21 is powered by the Snapdragon 888 (or Exynos 2100), which still handles everyday tasks, social media, and light gaming without issue in 2026.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 6.2-inch FHD+ Dynamic AMOLED 2X, 120Hz. The flat panel with adaptive refresh rate (48-120Hz) delivers smooth scrolling while saving battery.
- **Camera:** 12MP main (f/1.8, OIS) + 12MP ultra-wide + 64MP telephoto (3x hybrid zoom). The 64MP sensor enables high-resolution cropping that delivers surprisingly good zoom shots.
- **Battery:** 4,000mAh with 25W fast charging and 15W wireless charging. A full day is achievable with moderate use.
- **Software:** One UI 5.x with 4 years of OS updates (up to Android 16) and 5 years of security patches.

## Who Is the Galaxy S21 Actually For?

The Galaxy S21 is for anyone looking for a capable, compact flagship at a budget-friendly price. If you're upgrading from an S10 or older, the 120Hz display, improved cameras, and modern One UI experience will feel like a significant leap forward.

It's also a great choice as a secondary phone or a first smartphone for someone who wants a premium experience without the latest flagship price tag.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy S21 may be a few generations old, but its core experience — beautiful 120Hz display, versatile camera system, and smooth One UI performance — remains solid. The plastic back keeps it light, and the flat screen is a practical advantage. If you're buying on a budget in 2026, the S21 is still a very capable phone.
