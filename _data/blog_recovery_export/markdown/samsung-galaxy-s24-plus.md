## Design and Build: Flat Display, Premium Feel

The Galaxy S24+ marked a significant design shift — moving from curved to a flat display, which made screen protectors easier to apply and reduced accidental touches. The phone features an Armor Aluminum 2 frame and Gorilla Glass Victus 2 protection, giving it a solid, premium feel.

The back panel has a matte finish that resists fingerprints, and the individually housed rear cameras add a touch of elegance. At 196g, it feels substantial without being heavy.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy S24+ introduced Galaxy AI — Samsung's suite of artificial intelligence features that transformed the smartphone experience.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Galaxy AI:** Circle to Search, Live Translate, Note Assist, Photo Assist, and Chat Assist — AI-powered features that enhance productivity and creativity.
- **Display:** 6.7-inch QHD+ Dynamic AMOLED 2X, 120Hz. The QHD+ resolution delivers exceptional sharpness, and the 2,600-nit peak brightness is outstanding for outdoor use.
- **Chipset:** Exynos 2400 (global) or Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 (US).
- **Camera:** 50MP main + 12MP ultra-wide + 10MP telephoto (3x).
- **Battery:** 4,900mAh with 45W charging — the fastest charging on any Galaxy S+ model.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy S24+ is the best value in the S24 lineup. The QHD+ display, large battery with 45W charging, and Galaxy AI features make it an incredibly well-rounded flagship.
