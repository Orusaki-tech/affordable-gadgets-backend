## Overview

The Galaxy Z Fold 4 improved on its predecessor with a slimmer design, wider cover display, and better cameras. The 50MP main camera was a significant upgrade, and the Taskbar made multitasking more intuitive.

## Key Features

- **Display:** 7.6-inch (inner) + 6.2-inch (cover), 120Hz.
- **Camera:** 50MP + 12MP ultra-wide + 10MP telephoto (3x).
- **Chip:** Snapdragon 8+ Gen 1.
- **Battery:** 4,400mAh.

## The Verdict

A great entry point into foldable ownership at a more accessible price in 2026.
