## Design and Build: Budget Not Basic

The Galaxy M13 brought Samsung's modern design language to the budget segment with a clean Infinity-V display, a waterdrop notch, and a textured back panel that provided a secure grip. The phone felt well-constructed despite its plastic frame, and the side-mounted fingerprint sensor was fast and accurate.

Samsung retained the 3.5mm headphone jack and USB-C port, and the phone was available in several attractive colours including a striking blue option.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy M13 offered a compelling set of features for its price bracket.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Camera:** 50MP main + 5MP ultra-wide + 2MP macro + 2MP depth. The 50MP main sensor captured detailed daylight photos, and the quad-camera setup offered versatility.
- **Display:** 6.6-inch HD+ PLS LCD. The large display was good for media consumption.
- **Battery:** 5,000mAh with 15W charging. Excellent battery life that could easily last two days.
- **Processor:** Exynos 850.
- **Storage:** 32GB/64GB/128GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy M13 offered a strong camera and massive battery at a very accessible price. It was a solid budget option for anyone who prioritised camera versatility and battery life.
