## Design and Build: Compact, Premium, Refined

The Galaxy S23 is the compact flagship in Samsung's 2023 lineup. At 6.1 inches, it fits comfortably in one hand while still delivering a premium experience. The flat Dynamic AMOLED display is protected by Gorilla Glass Victus 2, and the Armor Aluminum frame gives it a solid feel.

The rear camera design is clean — three individually housed lenses floating on the back panel without a raised camera bump. The phone feels more refined than the S22, with improved ergonomics and a matte finish that resists fingerprints.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy S23 is powered by the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 for Galaxy — a custom chip that delivers exceptional performance and efficiency.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Chipset:** Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 for Galaxy — one of the fastest mobile chips, optimized specifically for Samsung's software.
- **Display:** 6.1-inch FHD+ Dynamic AMOLED 2X, 120Hz. The flat display is bright, vibrant, and protected by Gorilla Glass Victus 2.
- **Camera:** 50MP main with OIS + 12MP ultra-wide + 10MP telephoto (3x). The 50MP sensor captures detailed, well-exposed photos in any lighting condition.
- **Battery:** 3,900mAh with 25W charging. The more efficient chipset means better battery life than its predecessor.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy S23 is the compact Android phone to beat. The Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 for Galaxy delivers flagship performance, the 50MP camera with 3x telephoto is versatile, and the 6.1-inch display is perfect for one-handed use.
