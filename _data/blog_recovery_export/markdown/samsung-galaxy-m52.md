## Design and Build: Surprisingly Slim

The Galaxy M52 was a departure from the typically bulky M-series phones, offering a surprisingly slim 7.4mm profile that rivalled more expensive phones. The Infinity-O display with hole-punch camera and flat sides gave it a modern, sleek appearance.

At just 173g, it was remarkably lightweight for a phone with 5G connectivity. The back panel featured a clean, minimalist design with the camera housing neatly integrated.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy M52 offered 5G connectivity and a smooth 120Hz display.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 6.7-inch Super AMOLED Plus, 120Hz. The 120Hz refresh rate delivered fluid scrolling and animations.
- **Camera:** 64MP main + 12MP ultra-wide + 5MP macro + 5MP depth.
- **Battery:** 5,000mAh with 25W fast charging. Despite the slim design, Samsung packed in a large battery.
- **Processor:** Snapdragon 778G — a capable mid-range chip.
- **5G:** Full sub-6GHz 5G support.
- **Storage:** 128GB/256GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy M52 proved that a 5G phone with a large battery could be slim and lightweight. The 120Hz Super AMOLED display and Snapdragon 778G chip made it a compelling mid-range option.
