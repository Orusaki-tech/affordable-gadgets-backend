## Design and Build: A New Era for Samsung

The Galaxy S20 marked the start of a new naming scheme and design direction for Samsung. The phone featured a stunning Dynamic AMOLED 2X display with 120Hz refresh rate — a first for Samsung's flagship line — wrapped in a glass-and-metal construction that felt incredibly premium.

The rear camera module was a rectangular housing that held the triple-camera system, a design that became a Samsung signature. The phone was available in striking colours and weighed just 163g.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy S20 was powered by the Snapdragon 865 or Exynos 990, delivering smooth performance.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 6.2-inch QHD+ Dynamic AMOLED 2X, 120Hz. The 120Hz display was buttery smooth, though QHD+ resolution and 120Hz couldn't be used simultaneously.
- **Camera:** 12MP main + 64MP telephoto (3x hybrid zoom) + 12MP ultra-wide. The 64MP telephoto sensor enabled high-quality zoom and 8K video recording.
- **8K Video:** First Galaxy phone to support 8K video recording at 24fps.
- **Battery:** 4,000mAh with 25W charging.
- **Storage:** 128GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy S20 was a trendsetter that introduced 120Hz displays to the flagship world. In 2026, it's an older device but still capable for basic tasks and media consumption.
