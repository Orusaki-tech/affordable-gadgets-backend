## Design and Build: The First Pro iPhone

The iPhone 11 Pro was Apple's first 'Pro' branded iPhone, featuring a stainless steel frame, matte glass back, and a triple-camera system. The 5.8-inch Super Retina XDR display was stunning for its time, with 1200 nits peak brightness for HDR content.

## Key Features

- **Camera:** 12MP main + 12MP ultra-wide + 12MP telephoto (2x zoom). Night Mode was a game-changer when it launched.
- **Chip:** A13 Bionic — still usable for basic apps but showing its age.
- **Battery:** Up to 18 hours of video playback.
- **Display:** 5.8-inch Super Retina XDR OLED.

## The Verdict

The iPhone 11 Pro is now an ultra-budget option for those who want a premium iPhone camera experience. The A13 chip limits modern gaming and heavy apps, but for calls, messages, social media, and great photos, it still works.
