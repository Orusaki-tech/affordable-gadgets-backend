## Design and Build: Titanium Excellence

The iPhone 17 Pro continues Apple's use of Grade 5 titanium, now with a refined micro-blasted texture that improves grip. At 191g for the 6.3-inch model, it strikes an excellent balance between screen real estate and one-handed usability.

The camera bump has been redesigned with a unified glass cover that houses the triple-lens array, making it less protrusive than previous generations. The display bezels have been reduced further, giving the phone an even more immersive front face.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The iPhone 17 Pro is powered by the A19 Pro chip, which features an upgraded 6-core GPU with hardware-accelerated ray tracing that rivals dedicated gaming handhelds.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Camera:** 48MP main + 48MP ultra-wide + 12MP periscope telephoto with 6x optical zoom. The periscope lens is new to the Pro (previously Pro Max only), giving the smaller Pro serious zoom capabilities.
- **Display:** 6.3-inch LTPO OLED with 120Hz ProMotion, always-on display, and 2,200 nits peak brightness.
- **Battery:** Up to 27 hours of video playback — a solid improvement over the 16 Pro.
- **Connectivity:** Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 5.4, and Thread radio for smart home integration.

## Who Is the iPhone 17 Pro Actually For?

The iPhone 17 Pro is for the photography enthusiast who wants periscope zoom in a manageable size. The addition of 6x optical zoom to the smaller Pro body makes it far more versatile for travel, wildlife, and portrait photography.

## The Verdict

With the addition of periscope zoom, the A19 Pro chip, and a refined titanium design, the iPhone 17 Pro is the most well-rounded Pro iPhone Apple has ever made. It now offers everything the Pro Max offers in a more pocketable size.
