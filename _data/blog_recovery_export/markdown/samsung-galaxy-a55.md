## Design and Build: Almost Flagship

The Galaxy A55 represents the pinnacle of Samsung's A-series design. The flat Super AMOLED display with a hole-punch camera, aluminium frame (a first for the A-series), and Gorilla Glass Victus+ protection give it a look and feel that rivals flagship phones.

The Key Island design and IP67 water resistance complete the premium package. Samsung has also included stereo speakers with Dolby Atmos support.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy A55 is powered by the Exynos 1480 chipset, which offers excellent performance for gaming, multitasking, and media consumption.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 6.6-inch Super AMOLED, 120Hz, 1,000 nits. Vision Booster enhances outdoor visibility.
- **Camera:** 50MP main with OIS + 12MP ultra-wide + 5MP macro + 5MP depth. The 50MP OIS primary camera captures stunning detail.
- **Battery:** 5,000mAh with 45W fast charging — the fastest charging on any A-series phone.
- **Build:** Aluminium frame + Gorilla Glass Victus+.
- **IP Rating:** IP67 water and dust resistance.
- **Storage:** 128GB/256GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy A55 is the best A-series phone Samsung has ever made. The aluminium frame, 120Hz Super AMOLED display, 45W fast charging, and IP67 water resistance make it feel like a flagship in every way that matters.
