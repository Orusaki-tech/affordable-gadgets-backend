The Galaxy S23 Ultra was a landmark device — it introduced the 200MP camera sensor, the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 for Galaxy chip, and the integrated S Pen that Note fans loved.

The S23 Ultra was a camera powerhouse that still competes with current flagships. The 10x optical zoom remains impressive.
