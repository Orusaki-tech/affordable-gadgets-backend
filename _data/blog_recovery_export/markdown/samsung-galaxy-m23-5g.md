## Design and Build: 5G for the Masses

The Galaxy M23 5G brought next-generation connectivity to Samsung's budget M-series without sacrificing core features. The Infinity-V display with waterdrop notch and the clean back panel with a triple-camera housing gave it a familiar, modern Samsung look.

The side-mounted fingerprint sensor was fast and natural to use. The phone felt solid despite its plastic construction, and Samsung retained the 3.5mm headphone jack.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy M23 5G offered a strong balance of features for its price.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **5G:** Sub-6GHz 5G support for faster data speeds and future-proof connectivity.
- **Display:** 6.6-inch FHD+ PLS LCD, 120Hz. The 120Hz refresh rate delivered smooth scrolling and animations.
- **Camera:** 50MP main + 8MP ultra-wide + 2MP macro. The 50MP main sensor captured detailed photos.
- **Battery:** 5,000mAh with 25W fast charging. Reliable all-day battery life.
- **Processor:** Snapdragon 750G.
- **Storage:** 128GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy M23 5G was a solid budget 5G option that offered a 120Hz display, 50MP camera, and large battery. It was an excellent choice for anyone looking to experience 5G without spending a lot.
