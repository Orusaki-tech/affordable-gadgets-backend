## Overview

The iPhone 7 Plus was Apple's first dual-camera iPhone, introducing the beloved Portrait mode with bokeh effect. The 5.5-inch Retina HD display, IP67 water resistance, and the iconic jet black finish made it a classic.

## Key Features

- **Camera:** 12MP dual cameras with 2x optical zoom and Portrait mode.
- **Display:** 5.5-inch Retina HD display.
- **Chip:** A10 Fusion — very dated.

## The Verdict

A historic iPhone that pioneered dual-camera photography. In 2026, it's only suitable as a basic secondary phone.
