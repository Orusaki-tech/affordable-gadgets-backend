## Design and Build: Built to Last

The Galaxy M12 was designed with one primary focus: battery life. The massive 6,000mAh battery was the headline feature, but Samsung didn't neglect the rest of the package. The Infinity-V display with waterdrop notch and the textured back panel gave it a distinctive look.

The phone featured a side-mounted fingerprint sensor (integrated with the power button) and retained the 3.5mm headphone jack. At 221g, the weight was noticeably higher than average due to the large battery.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy M12 offered excellent value for battery-conscious buyers.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Battery:** 6,000mAh — the massive battery delivered exceptional endurance, easily lasting two to three days of moderate use.
- **Display:** 6.5-inch HD+ PLS LCD, 90Hz. The 90Hz refresh rate was a welcome surprise at this price.
- **Camera:** 48MP main + 5MP ultra-wide + 2MP macro + 2MP depth. The quad-camera setup offered versatility.
- **Processor:** Exynos 850.
- **Storage:** 32GB/64GB/128GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy M12 was the phone to get if battery life was your top priority. The 6,000mAh battery delivered incredible endurance, and the 90Hz display and 48MP camera were impressive extras at this price.
