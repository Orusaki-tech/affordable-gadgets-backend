## Design and Build: Samsung's Mid-Range Formula Perfected

The Galaxy A35 embodies Samsung's refined mid-range philosophy, packing features that were once exclusive to flagships into an accessible package. The flat Super AMOLED display with a U-shaped notch, Key Island button housing, and clean back panel with vertically aligned cameras give it a mature, modern look.

The phone features Gorilla Glass Victus+ on the front and an IP67 rating for water and dust resistance. Samsung has also included stereo speakers — a welcome upgrade for media consumption.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy A35 balances performance, display quality, and camera capability exceptionally well.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 6.6-inch Super AMOLED, 120Hz, 1,000 nits. Vision Booster technology enhances outdoor visibility.
- **Camera:** 50MP main with OIS + 8MP ultra-wide + 5MP macro + 2MP depth. The 50MP OIS camera captures sharp, detailed photos in various lighting conditions.
- **Battery:** 5,000mAh with 25W fast charging. Reliable all-day performance.
- **IP Rating:** IP67 — dust and water resistant.
- **Processor:** Exynos 1380 (5nm) — a capable mid-range chip with good efficiency.
- **Storage:** 128GB/256GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy A35 is one of the best mid-range phones Samsung offers. The 120Hz Super AMOLED display, 50MP OIS camera, IP67 water resistance, and stereo speakers make it a well-rounded package that's hard to beat at its price.
