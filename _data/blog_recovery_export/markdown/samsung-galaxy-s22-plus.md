## Design and Build: The Perfect Size?

The Galaxy S22+ struck a balance between the compact S22 and the massive S22 Ultra, offering a 6.6-inch flat Dynamic AMOLED 2X display that was ideal for both productivity and media consumption. The contour-cut camera housing and Armor Aluminum frame gave it a sleek, premium appearance.

The matte glass back resisted fingerprints, and the phone felt solid at 195g. IP68 water resistance provided peace of mind.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy S22+ offered the same core camera experience as the S22 with a larger battery and faster charging.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 6.6-inch FHD+ Dynamic AMOLED 2X, 120Hz. Flat display for easy screen protector application.
- **Camera:** 50MP main + 12MP ultra-wide + 10MP telephoto (3x). The 50MP sensor captured excellent photos with Samsung's advanced image processing.
- **Battery:** 4,500mAh with 45W charging — significantly faster than the base S22's 25W.
- **Chipset:** Snapdragon 8 Gen 1 / Exynos 2200.
- **Storage:** 128GB/256GB.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy S22+ was the best value in the S22 lineup, offering the same excellent camera system with a larger display and faster charging. In 2026, it's a capable flagship at a budget-friendly price.
