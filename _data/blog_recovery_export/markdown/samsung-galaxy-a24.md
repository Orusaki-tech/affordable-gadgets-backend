## Design and Build: Clean and Modern

The Galaxy A24 adopted Samsung's cleaner design language with a flat display, minimal bezels, and a vertically aligned triple-camera module. The plastic back had a subtle matte finish that felt surprisingly premium and resisted fingerprints well.

At 195g, it felt substantial without being heavy. The side-mounted fingerprint sensor was responsive, and Samsung retained both the 3.5mm headphone jack and USB-C port.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy A24 brought back the Super AMOLED display that budget fans loved.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 6.5-inch Super AMOLED, 90Hz, 1,000 nits. The Super AMOLED panel delivers excellent contrast, vibrant colours, and high brightness for outdoor use.
- **Camera:** 50MP main with OIS + 5MP ultra-wide + 2MP macro. OIS on the main camera helps capture sharper photos in low light and smoother video.
- **Battery:** 5,000mAh with 25W charging. Reliable all-day battery life with fast charging support.
- **Processor:** MediaTek Helio G99 — a capable mid-range chip.
- **Storage:** 128GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy A24 is one of the best-balanced budget phones Samsung offers. The Super AMOLED display is gorgeous, the 50MP OIS camera captures great photos, and the 5,000mAh battery delivers solid endurance. Strong value at its price point.
