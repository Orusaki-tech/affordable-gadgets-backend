## Design and Build: Stainless Steel Without the Flagship Price

The iPhone 12 Pro brought the premium stainless steel frame and matte glass back to the Pro lineup for the first time. It launched alongside the iPhone 12 and shared the same flat-edged design, but the polished steel bands and triple-camera system distinguished it as the premium option.

LiDAR scanner, ProRAW photos, and ProRes video recording made the 12 Pro a capable tool for creators.

## Key Features

- **Display:** 6.1-inch Super Retina XDR OLED with 800 nits max brightness.
- **Camera:** 12MP main + 12MP ultra-wide + 12MP telephoto (2x zoom). Night Mode on all lenses.
- **Chip:** A14 Bionic — handles everyday tasks smoothly.
- **LiDAR:** Faster autofocus in low light and improved AR experiences.

## The Verdict

In 2026, the iPhone 12 Pro offers a premium stainless steel build and a versatile triple-camera system at a very accessible price. The A14 chip is showing its age, but for basic use and great photos, it's still a solid budget iPhone.
