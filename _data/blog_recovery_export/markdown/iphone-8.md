## Overview

The iPhone 8 marked the end of an era — it was the last iPhone with the classic home button design before the iPhone X introduced Face ID. The glass back enabled wireless charging, and the A11 Bionic chip was a significant performance leap.

## Key Features

- **Display:** 4.7-inch Retina HD display with True Tone.
- **Camera:** 12MP main camera with OIS and Portrait Lighting.
- **Chip:** A11 Bionic — very dated but faster than A10.
- **Wireless Charging:** First iPhone with Qi wireless charging.

## The Verdict

In 2026, the iPhone 8 is an ultra-budget option with wireless charging — a rare feature at this price point. iOS support is limited.
