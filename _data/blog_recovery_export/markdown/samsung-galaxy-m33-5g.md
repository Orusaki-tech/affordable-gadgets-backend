## Design and Build: Future-Ready Budget

The Galaxy M33 5G brought a 120Hz display and 5G connectivity to Samsung's M-series, offering a smooth, future-proof experience at a budget price. The Infinity-V display with a waterdrop notch and the textured back panel with a distinctive camera housing made it instantly recognisable.

The side-mounted fingerprint sensor was reliable, and the phone felt well-constructed. Samsung retained the 3.5mm headphone jack despite the 5G hardware.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy M33 5G offered an impressive specification sheet for its price.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 6.6-inch FHD+ PLS LCD, 120Hz. The 120Hz refresh rate delivered exceptionally smooth scrolling.
- **Battery:** 6,000mAh with 25W fast charging. Massive capacity for multi-day endurance.
- **5G:** Sub-6GHz 5G support.
- **Camera:** 50MP main + 5MP ultra-wide + 2MP macro + 2MP depth.
- **Processor:** Exynos 1280 (5nm).
- **Storage:** 128GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy M33 5G was an excellent budget phone for 5G users who also wanted a smooth display and exceptional battery life. The 120Hz screen and 6,000mAh battery were standout features.
