## Design and Build: Battery-First Design

The Galaxy M30s was built for users who valued battery life above all else. The massive 6,000mAh battery defined the phone's character, delivering exceptional endurance that could easily last two to three days. The Infinity-U display with waterdrop notch and the glossy back panel gave it a familiar Samsung aesthetic.

At 188g, the phone was reasonably light for its battery capacity. The rear-mounted fingerprint sensor was positioned for comfortable access, and Samsung included both a 3.5mm headphone jack and USB-C port.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy M30s offered impressive battery life and a capable camera system.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Battery:** 6,000mAh with 15W fast charging. The defining feature — exceptional battery life that outperformed most phones.
- **Camera:** 48MP main + 8MP ultra-wide + 5MP depth. The 48MP main sensor captured detailed photos.
- **Display:** 6.4-inch Super AMOLED, FHD+. The AMOLED display delivered vibrant colours and deep blacks.
- **Processor:** Exynos 9611.
- **Storage:** 64GB/128GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy M30s was one of the best battery-life phones of its generation, combining a 6,000mAh battery with a Super AMOLED display. For anyone who hated running out of battery, this was a perfect choice.
