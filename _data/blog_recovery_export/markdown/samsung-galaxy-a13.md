## Design and Build: Familiar, Refined, Affordable

The Galaxy A13 builds on the A12's design with subtle refinements. The camera housing is cleaner, the back panel has a premium matte finish (on some colours), and the phone feels well-constructed despite its plastic build. At 195g, it's actually lighter than its predecessor.

The side-mounted fingerprint sensor returns, and Samsung has improved its speed and accuracy. The 3.5mm headphone jack is present, along with a USB-C port for charging.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy A13 was one of the first budget Samsung phones to feature a 90Hz display, making scrolling and animations noticeably smoother.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 6.6-inch FHD+ PLS LCD, 90Hz. The 90Hz refresh rate is a rare find at this price point and makes a real difference in everyday use.
- **Camera:** 50MP main + 5MP ultra-wide + 2MP macro + 2MP depth. The 50MP sensor with f/1.8 aperture captures decent photos in good lighting.
- **Battery:** 5,000mAh with 15W charging. Reliable all-day battery life.
- **Processor:** Exynos 850 or MediaTek Helio G80 depending on region.
- **Storage:** 32GB/64GB/128GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy A13 stands out in the budget segment thanks to its 90Hz display — a feature usually reserved for more expensive phones. Combined with the 50MP camera and 5,000mAh battery, it offers excellent value for anyone looking for an affordable Samsung smartphone.
