## Design and Build: Finally, a Nearly Crease-Free Display

The Galaxy Z Fold 5 was a major milestone in foldable technology. Samsung's new Flex Hinge design allowed the phone to close completely flat with no gap, and the screen crease was significantly reduced compared to previous generations. The phone felt noticeably thinner and lighter in hand.

Armor Aluminum frame and Gorilla Glass Victus 2 protection ensured durability. The IPX8 water resistance rating gave peace of mind for everyday use.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy Z Fold 5 was powered by the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 for Galaxy chip, matching the performance of the S23 series.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 7.6-inch QXGA+ Dynamic AMOLED 2X, 120Hz (inner) + 6.2-inch HD+ Dynamic AMOLED 2X, 120Hz (cover). Both displays are vibrant and smooth.
- **Chipset:** Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 for Galaxy.
- **Camera:** 50MP main + 12MP ultra-wide + 10MP telephoto (3x) — the same camera hardware as the Galaxy S23+.
- **S Pen Support:** Compatible with S Pen Fold Edition for note-taking and drawing on the large inner display.
- **Battery:** 4,400mAh with 25W charging.
- **Hinge:** New Flex Hinge with dual-rail structure for a tighter fold and reduced crease.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy Z Fold 5 is the most refined foldable Samsung has made. The nearly crease-free display, improved hinge, S Pen support, and flagship performance make it the best foldable for productivity.
