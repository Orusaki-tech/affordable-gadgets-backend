## Design and Build: The Ultimate M-Series

The Galaxy M54 represents the pinnacle of Samsung's M-series, combining a 108MP camera, 120Hz Super AMOLED display, and a massive 6,000mAh battery in a single package. The Infinity-O display with a hole-punch camera dominated the front, and the back panel featured a refined camera housing.

At 199g, the phone was well-balanced for its size and battery capacity. Samsung retained the 3.5mm headphone jack.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy M54 offered flagship-level features at a budget price.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Camera:** 108MP main + 8MP ultra-wide + 2MP macro. The 108MP sensor was the star of the show, capturing incredibly detailed photos with Samsung's advanced image processing.
- **Display:** 6.7-inch Super AMOLED Plus, 120Hz. Vibrant colours, deep blacks, and smooth scrolling.
- **Battery:** 6,000mAh with 25W fast charging. Exceptional endurance with large capacity.
- **Processor:** Exynos 1380 (5nm).
- **5G:** Sub-6GHz 5G support.
- **Storage:** 128GB/256GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy M54 was the best M-series phone Samsung ever made. The 108MP camera, 120Hz Super AMOLED display, and 6,000mAh battery made it a compelling choice for anyone seeking maximum value. It delivered flagship features at a fraction of the price.
