## Design and Build: Modern Galaxy Language

The Galaxy A25 follows Samsung's Key Island design philosophy, with a raised button housing on the right edge that makes the volume rocker and fingerprint sensor easy to locate by touch. The flat Super AMOLED display with a U-shaped notch dominates the front.

The back panel has a clean, minimalist design with a vertically aligned triple-camera setup. The plastic construction keeps the phone light at 197g, and the matte finish resists smudges.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy A25 marks a significant performance upgrade with the Exynos 1280 chipset.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Chipset:** Exynos 1280 (5nm) — the same chip found in the Galaxy A53, offering significantly better performance than the Helio G-series chips in previous A-series models.
- **Display:** 6.5-inch Super AMOLED, 120Hz, 1,000 nits. The 120Hz refresh rate delivers exceptionally smooth scrolling.
- **Camera:** 50MP main with OIS + 8MP ultra-wide + 2MP macro. OIS ensures sharp photos and stable video recording.
- **Battery:** 5,000mAh with 25W charging.
- **Storage:** 128GB/256GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy A25 brings a meaningful performance upgrade to the budget A-series. The Exynos 1280 chip provides smooth everyday performance, while the Super AMOLED 120Hz display and OIS camera round out an excellent package.
