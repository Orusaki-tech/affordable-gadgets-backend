## Design and Build: A Design Milestone

The Galaxy A30 was a significant phone in Samsung's A-series history. It was one of the first budget Samsung phones to feature a Super AMOLED display — a major differentiator that set it apart from LCD-equipped competitors. The Infinity-U display with its small waterdrop notch gave it a modern, flagship-inspired look.

The glossy plastic back with a subtle reflective pattern looked premium, and the rear-mounted fingerprint sensor was positioned ergonomically. At just 165g, it was impressively lightweight.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy A30 was powered by an Exynos 7904 processor with 3GB or 4GB of RAM.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 6.4-inch Super AMOLED, FHD+. The Super AMOLED panel delivered deep blacks, vibrant colours, and excellent viewing angles — easily the best display in its price segment at launch.
- **Camera:** 16MP main + 5MP ultra-wide. The dual-camera setup with ultra-wide angle photography was a rare feature at this price.
- **Battery:** 4,000mAh with 15W fast charging. Reliable battery life that could comfortably last a full day.
- **Storage:** 32GB/64GB, expandable via microSD.
- **Headphone Jack:** 3.5mm with Dolby Atmos support.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy A30 was a trailblazer that proved Super AMOLED quality could be accessible at budget prices. In 2026, it's a dated device, but it represents the beginning of Samsung's commitment to bringing premium display technology to every price bracket.
