## Design and Build: Big Phone, No Compromises

The iPhone 15 Plus delivers the large-screen iPhone experience without requiring a Pro budget. The 6.7-inch OLED display is the same size as the Pro Max, the aluminium frame is lightweight at 201g, and the colour-infused glass back adds a touch of personality.

USB-C charging is here, as is the Dynamic Island. The IP68 rating means it survives splashes and dunks. This is a big phone that doesn't feel cheap.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The A16 Bionic powers the iPhone 15 Plus, delivering smooth performance for all everyday tasks and most games.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Battery:** Up to 26 hours of video playback — the standout feature. The combination of the efficient A16 chip and the large chassis allows for a substantial battery that easily lasts two days for moderate users.
- **Display:** 6.7-inch Super Retina XDR OLED with 60Hz. Bright, vibrant, and perfect for Netflix.
- **Camera:** 48MP main + 12MP ultra-wide. The main sensor delivers excellent detail, and Photonic Engine processing enhances low-light shots.

## Who Is the iPhone 15 Plus Actually For?

The iPhone 15 Plus is for anyone who prioritises battery life and screen size above everything else. If you want to never worry about charging before an evening out, this phone delivers.

## The Verdict

In 2026, the iPhone 15 Plus is still one of the best-value large-screen phones you can buy. The battery life is exceptional, the display is gorgeous, and the A16 chip handles everything smoothly.
