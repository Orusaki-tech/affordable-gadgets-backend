## Overview

The iPhone 11 Pro Max was the first iPhone to carry the 'Pro Max' name, combining a 6.5-inch Super Retina XDR OLED display with Apple's first triple-camera system. The stainless steel frame and matte glass back defined the Pro aesthetic.

## Key Features

- **Display:** 6.5-inch Super Retina XDR OLED — excellent for media.
- **Camera:** 12MP triple camera with Night Mode.
- **Chip:** A13 Bionic — suitable for basic apps.
- **Battery:** Up to 20 hours of video playback.

## The Verdict

The iPhone 11 Pro Max is a great used option for anyone wanting a large-screen iPhone on a tight budget. The A13 isn't fast by today's standards, but the camera and display remain impressive.
