## Design and Build: 5G for the Masses

The Galaxy A14 was Samsung's effort to bring 5G connectivity to the budget segment without compromising on core features. The design follows the familiar Galaxy aesthetic with a flat display, minimal bezels, and a clean camera housing on the back.

The plastic back has a subtle grooved texture on certain colours that adds grip and resists fingerprints. At 202g, it's slightly heavier than the 4G models, likely due to the 5G modem hardware.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy A14 is available in both 4G (Exynos 850) and 5G (MediaTek Dimensity 700 or Exynos 1330) variants, with the 5G version offering significantly better performance.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Camera:** 50MP main + 5MP ultra-wide + 2MP macro. The 50MP main sensor delivers solid results in good light, with Samsung's image processing providing vibrant colours.
- **Display:** 6.6-inch FHD+ PLS LCD, 60Hz or 90Hz depending on variant. The FHD+ resolution is a welcome upgrade from the HD+ panels on older A-series models.
- **Battery:** 5,000mAh with 15W charging. The large battery compensates for the 5G modem's higher power draw.
- **5G:** Sub-6GHz 5G support for faster data speeds — future-proofing for Kenya's expanding 5G networks.
- **Storage:** 64GB/128GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy A14 is a well-rounded budget phone that brings 5G connectivity, a 50MP camera, and a large battery together in a familiar Samsung package. It's an excellent choice for anyone looking to future-proof their phone purchase without breaking the bank.
