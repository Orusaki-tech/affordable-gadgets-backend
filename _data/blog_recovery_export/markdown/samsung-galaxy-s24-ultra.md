## Overview

The Galaxy S24 Ultra was Samsung's first titanium-framed Ultra, replacing the Note legacy with a refined design. It introduced Galaxy AI, a flat 6.8-inch display, and a 200MP quad-camera system with a new 50MP 5x periscope lens.

## Key Features

- **Camera:** 200MP main + 50MP ultra-wide + 10MP telephoto (3x) + 50MP periscope (5x).
- **Display:** 6.8-inch QHD+ Dynamic AMOLED 2X, 120Hz, flat.
- **Chip:** Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 for Galaxy.
- **Build:** Titanium frame, Gorilla Glass Armor.
- **S Pen:** Built-in with Air Actions.
- **AI:** Circle to Search, Live Translate, Note Assist, Transcript Assist.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy S24 Ultra remains one of the best Android phones. The titanium build, Galaxy AI, and S Pen productivity make it a compelling choice even in 2026.
