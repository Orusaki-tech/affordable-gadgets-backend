## Design and Build: Premium Budget Design

The Galaxy M53 was a standout in the M-series for its 108MP camera — a sensor typically found on much more expensive phones. The Infinity-O display with hole-punch camera and the slim 7.4mm profile gave it a premium appearance that belied its price.

The back panel featured a large camera housing that housed the quad-camera system, including the prominent 108MP sensor. The phone was lightweight at 176g despite its large battery.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy M53 offered flagship-level camera resolution at a budget price.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Camera:** 108MP main + 8MP ultra-wide + 2MP macro + 2MP depth. The 108MP sensor captured incredibly detailed photos, though the pixel-binned 12MP output was more practical for everyday use.
- **Display:** 6.7-inch Super AMOLED Plus, 120Hz. Smooth, vibrant, and immersive.
- **Battery:** 5,000mAh with 25W fast charging.
- **Processor:** MediaTek Dimensity 900.
- **5G:** Sub-6GHz 5G support.
- **Storage:** 128GB/256GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy M53 offered exceptional camera resolution and display quality at a budget price. The 108MP camera, 120Hz Super AMOLED display, and 5G connectivity made it one of the best-value phones in its segment.
