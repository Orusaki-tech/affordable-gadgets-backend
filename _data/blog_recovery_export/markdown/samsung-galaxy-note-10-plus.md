## Design and Build: The Modern Note Design Language

The Galaxy Note 10+ introduced the centred hole-punch camera and the sleek, angular design language that defined Samsung's 2019-2020 era. The 6.8-inch Dynamic AMOLED display with minimal bezels was the star of the show, and the S Pen was housed in its familiar silo.

The Aura Glow colour was Instagram-famous for its rainbow-like reflective finish. The phone was undeniably premium, with a glass sandwich and metal frame.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Note 10+ was a productivity powerhouse, featuring Samsung DeX through a USB-C connection, the S Pen with Air Actions, and a 4,300mAh battery with 45W fast charging.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 6.8-inch WQHD+ Dynamic AMOLED, HDR10+. Excellent quality even by modern standards.
- **S Pen:** Bluetooth-enabled with gesture controls (Air Actions) for presentations, camera shutter, and music control.
- **Camera:** 12MP main + 16MP ultra-wide + 12MP telephoto (2x) + ToF 3D depth sensor.
- **DeX:** Desktop experience via USB-C — connect to a monitor, keyboard, and mouse.
- **Battery:** 4,300mAh with 45W charging.

## The Verdict

The Note 10+ in 2026 offers the full S Pen experience and DeX productivity at a very accessible price. The chip is dated, but for note-taking, document editing, and media consumption, it remains capable. The screen is still gorgeous.
