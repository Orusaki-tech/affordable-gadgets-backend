## Design and Build: The Complete Package

The Galaxy S20+ was the Goldilocks phone of Samsung's 2020 lineup — larger than the S20 but not as massive as the S20 Ultra. The 6.7-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X display with 120Hz refresh rate was stunning, and the glass-metal construction felt premium.

The quad-camera module with a dedicated ToF depth sensor sat in a rectangular housing on the back. The phone weighed 186g, well-balanced for its size.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy S20+ offered a complete flagship package for its generation.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 6.7-inch QHD+ Dynamic AMOLED 2X, 120Hz. Large, vibrant, and smooth.
- **Camera:** 12MP main + 64MP telephoto (3x hybrid zoom) + 12MP ultra-wide + ToF depth sensor. The quad-camera system was versatile for any shooting scenario.
- **5G:** Sub-6GHz and mmWave 5G support for future-proof connectivity.
- **Battery:** 4,500mAh with 25W charging. Excellent battery life for a big-screen phone.
- **Storage:** 128GB/256GB/512GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy S20+ was one of the most well-rounded phones of 2020. The 120Hz display, quad cameras, 5G, and large battery made it a true flagship. In 2026, it's a dated but capable device.
