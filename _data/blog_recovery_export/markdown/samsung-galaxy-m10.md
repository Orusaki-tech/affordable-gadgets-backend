## Design and Build: Affordable Reliability

The Galaxy M10 was Samsung's entry point into the M-series lineup, designed for the budget-conscious buyer who wanted Samsung quality without the premium price. The Infinity-V display with a waterdrop notch gave it a modern look, and the plastic construction kept it lightweight at just 163g.

The phone featured a textured back panel that provided a secure grip and resisted fingerprints. Samsung retained the 3.5mm headphone jack.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy M10 was powered by an Exynos 7870 processor, which handled basic tasks adequately.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 6.22-inch HD+ PLS LCD, Infinity-V. The display offered good colour reproduction for its price segment.
- **Camera:** 13MP main + 5MP ultra-wide. The dual-camera setup with ultra-wide was a nice addition at this price.
- **Battery:** 3,400mAh. Adequate for light to moderate daily use.
- **Storage:** 32GB, expandable via microSD.
- **Processor:** Exynos 7870.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy M10 was a dependable budget phone that delivered the core Samsung experience at a very accessible price point. In 2026, it's a basic device suitable for calls, messaging, and light media use.
