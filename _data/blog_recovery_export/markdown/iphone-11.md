## Overview

The iPhone 11 was Apple's best-selling model for years, and for good reason. It delivered the excellent dual-camera system from the iPhone 11 Pro in a more affordable aluminium body. The 6.1-inch Liquid Retina LCD display was bright and colour-accurate.

## Key Features

- **Display:** 6.1-inch Liquid Retina LCD — not OLED, but still good.
- **Camera:** 12MP dual cameras (main + ultra-wide) with Night Mode.
- **Chip:** A13 Bionic — getting old, but handles basic apps.
- **Battery:** Up to 17 hours of video playback.

## The Verdict

In 2026, the iPhone 11 is an ultra-budget option. It runs iOS reliably for calls, messages, social media, and casual photos. If your budget is very tight and you want iOS, this is the most affordable iPhone we carry.
