## Design and Build: Flagship-Inspired Mid-Range

The Galaxy A56 continues Samsung's trajectory of bringing flagship features to the mid-range. The flat Super AMOLED display with a hole-punch camera, aluminium frame, and Gorilla Glass Victus+ give it a premium feel. The Key Island button housing is both functional and stylish.

IP67 water resistance, stereo speakers with Dolby Atmos, and a clean back panel with vertically aligned cameras round out the premium package.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy A56 offers impressive specifications that blur the line between mid-range and flagship.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 6.6-inch Super AMOLED, 120Hz, 1,000 nits. Bright, smooth, and vibrant.
- **Camera:** 50MP main with OIS + 12MP ultra-wide + 5MP macro + 5MP depth.
- **Battery:** 5,000mAh with 45W fast charging — one of the fastest charging speeds on any Samsung phone.
- **Build:** Aluminium frame with Gorilla Glass Victus+.
- **IP Rating:** IP67 water and dust resistance.
- **Processor:** Exynos 1480.
- **Storage:** 128GB/256GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy A56 delivers a near-flagship experience at a mid-range price. The 45W fast charging is a standout feature, and the premium build quality, smooth display, and capable camera make it an excellent all-rounder.
