## Design and Build: Key Island Design

The Galaxy A15 introduced Samsung's "Key Island" design — a raised area on the right edge that houses the volume rocker and side-mounted fingerprint sensor, making them easier to locate by feel. The flat 6.5-inch display with a U-shaped notch and slim bezels gives it a modern look.

The back panel has a clean, minimalist design with a vertically aligned triple-camera module. The plastic construction keeps the weight at a manageable 200g.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy A15 stands out in its segment with a Super AMOLED display — a significant upgrade from the LCD panels typically found at this price point.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 6.5-inch Super AMOLED, 90Hz, 800 nits. The Super AMOLED panel delivers deep blacks, vibrant colours, and excellent viewing angles — a rare find in this price bracket.
- **Camera:** 50MP main + 5MP ultra-wide + 2MP macro. The 50MP main camera with f/1.8 aperture captures solid daytime shots.
- **Battery:** 5,000mAh with 25W charging. The 25W charging is faster than most budget phones and significantly quicker than the 15W charging on cheaper A-series models.
- **Processor:** MediaTek Helio G99 (4G) or Dimensity 6100+ (5G variant).
- **Storage:** 128GB/256GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy A15 is one of the best budget phones Samsung has made. The Super AMOLED display alone justifies the price, and the 90Hz refresh rate, 25W charging, and 50MP camera round out an exceptional value package.
