## Overview

The Galaxy S24+ upgrades the S24 formula with a QHD+ resolution display and a larger 4,900mAh battery while keeping the same Galaxy AI features and camera system.

- **Display:** 6.7-inch QHD+ LTPO AMOLED, 120Hz.
- **Battery:** 4,900mAh with 45W fast charging.
- **Camera:** 50MP + 12MP ultra-wide + 10MP telephoto (3x).
- **Chip:** Exynos 2400 / Snapdragon 8 Gen 3.
- **AI:** Galaxy AI suite with Live Translate and Circle to Search.

The Galaxy S24+ is the sweet spot — QHD+ display, big battery, and Galaxy AI at a reasonable price.
