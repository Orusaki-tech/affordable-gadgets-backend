## Design and Build: Premium Lite

The Galaxy S10 Lite was a unique entry in Samsung's lineup, offering flagship-level performance at a mid-range price. Unlike the S10e which was a compact variant, the S10 Lite was a larger phone with a 6.7-inch display, designed for users who wanted big-screen immersion on a budget.

The phone featured a flat display (no curved edges), a hole-punch camera, and a distinctive camera housing with a glossy finish. The glass front and plastic back kept the weight manageable at 186g.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Galaxy S10 Lite was powered by the Snapdragon 855 — the same flagship chip found in the Galaxy S10 series.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Chipset:** Snapdragon 855 — flagship-level performance at a mid-range price.
- **Display:** 6.7-inch FHD+ Super AMOLED Plus. The large, vibrant display was perfect for media consumption.
- **Camera:** 48MP main with Super Steady OIS + 12MP ultra-wide + 5MP macro. The 48MP main sensor with Super Steady OIS captured smooth video and sharp photos.
- **Battery:** 4,500mAh with 45W super-fast charging — faster than the flagship S10.
- **Storage:** 128GB, expandable via microSD.

## The Verdict

The Galaxy S10 Lite was a smart buy for anyone who wanted flagship performance and a large display without paying flagship prices. The 45W charging was ahead of its time.
