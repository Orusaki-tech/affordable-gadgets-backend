## Design and Build: Note Features, Smaller Package

The Galaxy Note 10 was the smaller sibling of the Note 10+, offering the same S Pen experience in a more manageable 6.3-inch body. It shared the same centred hole-punch design and the iconic Aura Glow colour.

At 168g, it was one of the lightest Note phones ever made. The USB-C-only port (no headphone jack) was controversial, but it made room for the larger battery.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The Note 10 retained the core Note experience while making some compromises compared to the Plus model.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 6.3-inch FHD+ Dynamic AMOLED, HDR10+.
- **S Pen:** Bluetooth-enabled with Air Actions.
- **Camera:** 12MP main + 16MP ultra-wide + 12MP telephoto (2x). No ToF sensor.
- **Battery:** 3,500mAh with 25W charging.
- **Processor:** Snapdragon 855 / Exynos 9825.

## The Verdict

The Note 10 in 2026 is an affordable entry into the Note ecosystem. The S Pen and DeX remain unique features, but the processor is dated. Best suited as a secondary productivity device or a first smartphone with stylus support.
