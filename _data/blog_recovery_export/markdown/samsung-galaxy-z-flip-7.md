## Overview

The Galaxy Z Flip 7 is Samsung's most refined clamshell foldable. It folds into a compact square that fits any pocket and unfolds to a 6.7-inch AMOLED display. The Flex Mode allows hands-free video calls and photos.

## Key Features

- **Display:** 6.7-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X (inner), 3.4-inch Super AMOLED (cover), 120Hz.
- **Camera:** 50MP + 12MP ultra-wide.
- **Chip:** Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 4.
- **Battery:** 4,000mAh.
- **Cover Screen:** Full-featured with widgets, camera viewfinder, and quick replies.

## The Verdict

The Z Flip 7 is the most fashionable foldable — compact when folded, a full flagship when open.
