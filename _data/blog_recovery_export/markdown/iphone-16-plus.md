## Design and Build: The Big iPhone, Simplified

The iPhone 16 Plus shares the same design language as its smaller sibling — the vertical camera bump, the colour-infused glass back, and the aluminium frame — but stretched to a spacious 6.7 inches. It's a large phone, there's no getting around that, but at 199g it's noticeably lighter than the Pro Max models thanks to the aluminium frame.

The Plus has become Apple's best-selling large-screen option for a simple reason: it offers the same expansive display real estate as the Pro Max at a significantly lower price. If you want a big screen for media consumption without paying for Pro-tier cameras and titanium, this is your phone.

## Key Features Under the Hood

Under the hood, the iPhone 16 Plus runs the same A18 chip as the standard iPhone 16, delivering identical performance for everyday tasks and gaming.

Here is a quick look at the standout specs:

- **Display:** 6.7-inch Super Retina XDR OLED with 60Hz refresh rate. The larger canvas makes it ideal for watching movies, editing photos, and browsing social media.
- **Battery:** Up to 27 hours of video playback — the biggest battery in the non-Pro lineup. This is the phone's strongest selling point. Heavy users will comfortably get through a full day.
- **Camera:** 48MP main + 12MP ultra-wide, same as the iPhone 16. Excellent photos in good light, with Apple's computational photography handling tricky scenes automatically.
- **USB-C:** Fast transfers and universal charging with any USB-C cable you already own.

## Who Is the iPhone 16 Plus Actually For?

The iPhone 16 Plus is for anyone who prioritises battery life and screen size above all else. If you stream hours of video daily, read documents on your phone, or simply hate charging midday, this phone delivers.

It's also a great choice for older users or anyone who finds the smaller iPhone 16 screen too cramped for comfortable reading and typing.

## The Verdict

The iPhone 16 Plus is the smart choice for the big-phone buyer who doesn't need a Pro. You get the same excellent battery life and screen size as the Pro Max, the same A18 chip for future-proof performance, and a camera that handles most situations beautifully. The only compromise is the 60Hz display and the lack of a telephoto lens — but for the price difference, it's a trade worth making.
