## Overview

The iPhone XR was Apple's most popular iPhone for years, and for good reason. It delivered Face ID, the A12 Bionic chip, and excellent battery life in a colourful, affordable package. The Liquid Retina LCD display was the best LCD Apple ever made, with True Tone and wide colour gamut.

## Key Features

- **Display:** 6.1-inch Liquid Retina LCD with True Tone.
- **Camera:** 12MP single camera with Portrait mode (software-based).
- **Chip:** A12 Bionic.
- **Battery:** Up to 16 hours of video playback — excellent for its class.
- **Colours:** Available in a range of fun colours including Product(RED), Blue, Coral, and Yellow.

## The Verdict

The iPhone XR was the best-selling iPhone for a reason. In 2026, it's an ultra-budget option with Face ID and a large, colourful display. The A12 chip limits modern app performance, but for basic use it still works.
