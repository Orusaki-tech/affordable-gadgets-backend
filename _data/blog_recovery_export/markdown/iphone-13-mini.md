## Design and Build: Small Phone, Big Heart

The iPhone 13 mini remains the last truly compact flagship smartphone from Apple. The 5.4-inch display makes it one of the few phones that can be comfortably used one-handed. At 140g, it's incredibly portable.

## Key Features Under the Hood

The A15 Bionic chip delivers the same flagship performance as the larger iPhone 13, just in a smaller package.

- **Display:** 5.4-inch Super Retina XDR OLED — perfect for one-handed use.
- **Camera:** 12MP dual cameras with sensor-shift OIS.
- **Battery:** Up to 17 hours of video playback — the trade-off for the compact size.

## Who Is It For?

The iPhone 13 mini is for anyone who prioritises portability and one-handed usability. If you miss the days when phones fit comfortably in one hand, this is your last chance.

## The Verdict

The iPhone 13 mini is a niche gem. It delivers flagship performance in the most compact iPhone package. If you value size above all else, grab one while they're still available.
